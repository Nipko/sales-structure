# Navigation, menu, and Settings

The sidebar organizes Parallly by purpose: **Essentials**, **AI & Growth**, **Operations**, **Insights**, and **Administration**. **Settings** stays at the bottom so it always has a stable location. Visible options can vary by role, plan, and industry.

## Move around without losing context

- Select a section name to collapse or expand it.
- Use the command search with `Ctrl+K` or `⌘K` to open a screen by name.
- Favorite frequently used pages; recent items help you return to your previous work.
- Inside **Settings**, use the back control or visible path to return to the settings index. The browser Back button should also return to the previous screen, not the dashboard.

If an option is missing, first check your role, business vertical, and the features enabled under **Settings → Plan & Billing**. Do not change a setting only to make it appear without confirming with an administrator.

## Settings: "Essentials for your agent"

The **Settings** screen starts with an **Essentials for your agent** strip: the settings that actually change how the agent answers, at the top and without hunting for them.

- **Business information** — what your business does, address, phone, and contact email.
- **Business hours** — when the business is open (appointment availability is defined separately, under Appointments).
- **Team** — who receives the chats when the agent hands a conversation to a person. It takes you to the **Users** screen in the sidebar.
- **Setup assistant** — reopens the **Meet your agent** wizard at the step you left, losing nothing.

The remaining sections follow below. The technical items (webhooks, MCP, API keys) stay collapsed under **For developers**: if you have never opened it, you do not need it.

## Help on every screen and "Show me how"

Almost every screen has a **Help** panel: what this screen is, in two sentences, and the steps in the real order of the clicks. When a tour exists for that screen, the panel also shows a **Show me how** button that starts the guided tour: it highlights, step by step, where each thing is done. The tour **does not change** any setting; it only takes you to the exact place and you decide.

The setup tour anchors itself to visible menu items. If the sidebar is collapsed, open it before restarting the tour so every step remains aligned.

# Tools and modules for your industry

When you create the account you choose an **Industry** and a **Business type**. The modules in the menu are resolved from both, together with the account's effective capabilities, not only from the industry name. The product contains 20 industries and 80 canonical business types (72 selectable and 8 on a waitlist); 18 industries currently have at least one selectable business type. Event planning and construction remain registered to preserve their identity and product evolution, but their current business types are waitlisted and are not offered during new-account setup.

Industries with a current offer are healthcare, fashion and beauty (called "Beauty & aesthetics" in the sign-up form), real estate, restaurants, automotive, tourism, education, finance, professional services, retail, technology, veterinary, fitness, insurance, home services, pet services, photography, and other.

## Business types you cannot choose today

- **Waitlisted (8)**: Wedding planning (event planning), General contractor (construction), Property developer, Payments & collections (finance), Marketplace / E-commerce (retail), IT support & MSP (technology), Insurance carrier and Health insurance specialist (insurance). They exist as business types, but their full operation is still closed and sign-up does not offer them.
- **Existing accounts only (5)**: Construction & development (real estate), Fintech (finance), IT Consulting (technology), Pet grooming under veterinary (now Pet services → Pet grooming) and Wedding planner (photography). An account that already had one keeps working; a new account cannot choose them. In addition, old boutique and pet-shop accounts now show as Retail → Fashion & clothing, and old delivery accounts as Restaurants → Fast food.

## Where the modules appear

The sidebar groups the modules for your business type into two sections:

- **Daily work**: the records you operate every day (appointments or bookings, orders, cases, requests). Administrators, supervisors, and agents see them.
- **Catalogue & resources**: the data that configures that work (inventory, menu, courses, properties, packages). Administrators and supervisors see them.

A module's name changes with the industry and the business type. The appointments module can read **Appointments**, **Schedule**, **Bookings**, or **Reservations**; a car workshop sees **Work orders** instead of "Workshop orders"; and the **CRM** and the funnel are renamed too (for example **Patients** and **Patient Journey** in healthcare). It is the same module under another label: when this help uses the generic name, look for the equivalent in your menu.

## Modules by industry

- **Healthcare**: **Appointments** in every type except pharmacy. Dental, dermatology and aesthetic medicine, and psychology add **Treatment plans**; general medicine uses appointments only. Pharmacy uses **Orders** and **Inventory** (shown as "Products").
- **Fashion and beauty**: **Appointments**; spa and aesthetics centre add **Treatment plans**.
- **Real estate**: **Appointments** (for viewings) and **Listings**, which can read "Properties".
- **Restaurants**: **Orders** (food) and **Menu**. Casual dining and cafe add the table schedule (**Bookings**); fast food and dark kitchen do not use it.
- **Automotive**: the dealership uses **Appointments** and **Vehicles**. The car workshop uses **Workshop orders** (shown as "Work orders") and **Appointments**. Parts uses **Orders** and **Inventory** ("Parts"). Vehicle rental uses **Rentals & stays** (shown as "Reservations") and **Vehicles** ("Fleet").
- **Tourism**: hotel and vacation rental use **Stays** and **Properties** (shown as "Rooms"). Travel agency and tours use **Tour bookings** and **Tours & Packages** ("Packages" or "Tours").
- **Education**: **Appointments** and **Courses**, in all eight types (languages, university, online courses, corporate training, dance academy, music or art academy, private lessons, and driving school).
- **Finance**: advisory and credit use **Appointments**.
- **Professional services**: lawyers, accountants, architects, and consultants use **Cases** (the firm's matters) and **Appointments**.
- **Retail**: **Orders** and **Inventory** ("Products"); home and decor also adds **Appointments** and **Packages & services**.
- **Technology**: SaaS and software development use **Appointments**; hardware and networking uses **Orders** and **Inventory** ("Devices").
- **Veterinary**: **Appointments** (shown as "Schedule") and **Pets**.
- **Fitness**: **Memberships**, **Classes**, and the booking schedule (**Bookings**).
- **Insurance**: **Insurance** (shown as "Policies") for broker, life, and auto.
- **Home services**: **Requests** (shown as "Service requests") and **Packages & services**.
- **Pet services**: grooming, walks, and training use the booking schedule and **Pets**; daycare and dog hotel use **Rentals & stays** (shown as "Stays"), **Pets**, and **Packages & services**.
- **Photography**: **Photo sessions** (shown as "Sessions"; "Photo sessions" for wedding photography) and **Packages & services**.
- **Other**: **Orders** and **Inventory**.

**Packages & services** is the catalogue of what the business sells, with its duration, price, and capacity, for activities that are not booked by time slots. The **Stays**, **Tour bookings**, **Workshop orders**, and **Cases** screens are daily-work records: that is where you operate stays, tour departures, workshop jobs, and case files.

Not every business of a type receives every module: effective capabilities, business type, role, and account configuration control visibility. Selecting a type does not certify professional or regulatory compliance. If something is missing, an administrator should review **Settings → Business information** and **Plan & Billing** before contacting support. To see which agent tools belong to each business type, read the article **Agent tools for your business type**.

### Your industry's recipe

Everything that appears already written when you turn on an industry comes from a **recipe**: the agent's name and greeting, the frequently asked questions, the services with their example price, the vocabulary of the trade, and the reasons the agent hands a conversation to a person.

Not every industry has its own recipe. Only six do: fashion and beauty, healthcare, restaurants, retail, education, and home services; education also adds a layer per business type for dance and music or art academies, private lessons, and driving schools. The other industries start from the base definition, with no recipe. If you choose **Other**, Parallly proposes a recipe written with AI from the sentence you entered at sign-up about what your business does; it appears as a suggestion and never replaces what you already changed. If generation fails, the account keeps the generic definition.

A recipe **never invents a fact about your business**. It does not know your address, your phone, your payment methods or how many hours ahead someone can cancel. Where that fact would go it leaves a space like `[dirección]` or `[precio]` for you to fill. An invented fact that sounds plausible is worse than an empty space: the space is visible and gets filled; the invention goes out over WhatsApp.

# Parallly mobile app

The mobile app focuses daily work into four tabs: **Inbox**, **CRM**, **Operation**, and **More**. The Operation label adapts to the business vertical and may appear as Appointments, Stays, Tours, Orders, Classes, or another specialized workspace.

- **Inbox** lets users search and filter conversations, open and reply to them, start a conversation, and use available actions such as assign, snooze, resolve, or reopen. A chat may also expose attachments, saved replies, macros, notes, AI assistance, and reassignment.
- **CRM** lets users view and create contacts, scan a business card, update tags, notes, and tasks, and open the **Pipeline** to review opportunities, move stages, save notes, or assign them when the role allows it.
- **Operation** displays the workflow enabled for the business industry and subtype. Cards, statuses, actions, and creation flows depend on the account's effective capabilities and the user's role; a module existing on the platform does not mean it is enabled in that app or account.
- **More** brings together agent availability, pending tasks, operating metrics, language, notification preferences, privacy and account links, and sign out.

Notifications can open the related conversation and, when supported by the operating system, offer a quick reply. While offline, the app may display a recent Inbox copy and queue text replies to retry after reconnection; check the send state before assuming a message was delivered. Most other reads and changes require a connection.

Administrative setup for the company, channels, AI agents, security, billing, policies, and integrations remains on the web platform. Installation, updates, or app availability depend on your organization's distribution channel; this article does not confirm any app-store publication status.

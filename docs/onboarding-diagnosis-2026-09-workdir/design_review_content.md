# REVIEW — Recipes (vertical content) and the examples

## Verdict
The design's promise ("confirm examples, never a blank sheet") is right, but §3 overstates what the registry has and under-specifies what must be written. Reality in vertical-definitions.ts: 17 of 18 industries have a name, greeting, 3-9 services and 5 FAQs; ZERO have instructions, "when it doesn't know" options, recommended channels, test prompts or conversation examples; only 4 industries vary anything by subtype (the other ~60 subtypes change a label only). The biggest risk is D10: services are seeded today with hard COP prices and no example flag, and the booking engine lists them verbatim to customers, so "Usar así" on the price card would make the agent quote invented prices on day 0, the exact opposite of "we know the business". Fixable, but it is a schema change plus ~1,700 strings of new content, not "completing" a recipe.

## Strengths
- The step anatomy (question title, one-line why, full example, chips, Usar así / Después) is the right container for recipe content and matches what the registry can already feed (terminology, name, greeting, services, FAQs, hours, handoff triggers, pipeline).
- The 'blank, never invented' rule for generated recipes (§3.2, D9) is consistent with the existing seed policy in the file header of vertical-definitions.ts and with commit 95f758f3.
- Choosing 6 high-demand industries first (D13, §9) matches the deep-dive verdicts: belleza, salud and restaurantes are the three 'INVERTIR' verticals with the richest existing content to build on.
- The three worked examples pick the right archetypes (booking with classes, booking with clinical guardrails, orders+reservations) and the dental one already aligns with the registry's Sofía persona and handoff triggers.
- Measuring 'Usar así vs Cambiar' per card (§11) is the correct feedback loop for recipe quality and cheap to instrument.

### G2 [blocking] D10 is unimplementable as written: seed services carry hard prices with no 'example' flag
- where: §2 rule 'Un precio de ejemplo nunca llega a un cliente' and §10 D10
- why: The design claims the rule 'ya existe en la siembra de FAQs'. It exists for FAQs only. The registry seeds 37 services with concrete prices (salud 80.000/120.000/50.000 COP, belleza 40.000/120.000/50.000, serv_prof 250.000…) and the file header says services 'are listed verbatim over WhatsApp by the booking engine'. Nothing in the services table or in list_services distinguishes example from confirmed. If step 2 card 1 is 'Usar así' with example prices, the agent quotes them to real customers on day 0.
- PROPOSAL: Exact rule: (1) add services.price_status ENUM('example','confirmed','on_request') NOT NULL DEFAULT 'confirmed' (expand-only migration); bootstrap writes 'example' for every seeded price and 'on_request' for price 0. (2) list_services/check_availability return price only when 'confirmed'; for 'example' the tool returns price:null + priceNote:'pending_confirmation' and the L2 persona gets one fixed sentence: 'Sí tenemos [servicio]. El valor exacto te lo confirmo en un momento; mientras tanto, ¿te cuento horarios o te reservo?'. For 'on_request': 'Ese servicio se cotiza según tu caso; te paso con alguien del equipo que te da el valor.' (3) UI: price shown in dashed outline with a grey pill 'ejemplo' after it; tapping the price opens inline edit and saves as 'confirmed'; a second control per card 'Estos precios ya son los míos' confirms all at once; card footer counter 'Precios sin confirmar: 2 de 3'. (4) 'Usar así' with any example price shows one line under the button: 'Tus precios quedan como ejemplo: {Nombre} no los dirá hasta que los confirmes.' and the health panel shows orange 'No sabe precios (2 de 3)'. (5) Currency comes from the tenant's country at signup, never the hard-coded 'COP' of the seed; example amounts are stored per country (CO/MX/AR/PE/CL/BR) or shown unit-less ('desde —') outside the ones we have.
- evidence: vertical-definitions.ts:8-15 (header), :92-94, :180-182, :255-256; grep 'price:' = 37 hits, 0 hits for any example/confirmed flag in apps/api/src

### G8 [major] No content pipeline: recipes live inline in a 1,360-line TS file with no lint and lossy localisation
- where: §9 row 'Bootstrap por industria' and D13
- why: D13 assigns '18 × ~15 campos × 4 idiomas' to 'contenido de producto' but the storage is TypeScript, subtype content goes through verticals.service.ts branches, and the dossiers found that localizeVerticalTemplates collapses to templates[0] outside es (the Brazilian pet shop gets the groomer; technology loses BANT rules). - PROPOSAL: (1) One JSON per (industry, subtype, lang) under apps/api/src/modules/verticals/recipes/, loaded and validated at boot against a zod schema of §3.1. ### G10 [minor] 'Cómo te compran' offers four mechanisms but the recipe has only bookingEnabled
